## The batches

**M1 — the rethinks.** THE MANTA (the leap off its back at the breach, a
second manta in company, the breach framed and slowed; the record becomes
the leap); THE PHO RUN (the horn parts the traffic, the kerb spills a bowl,
cold pho ends the run, the stall re-arms it; the third drop is a moment);
THE CONCERT (the house answers every note, each note calls wider, the gulls
and the ferry answer, the encore has fireworks over the harbour and its own
camera); THE PARTY BUS (three banners to sidestep, the tally of ten, the
bus lit as night falls, the perfect run's solo).

**M2 — the birds and the boats.** THE CONDOR (the dive marked: roar,
slow-mo, camera, a plume ring over the crater); THE FRIGATEBIRD (the
landmark chain — Sugarloaf, the Redentor, the arches, without landing);
THE FERRY (a humpback off the Heads, framed from the deck; the wharf waves
you in); THE RIVER RUN (gates are seconds off the clock; the mill's
finish framed).

**M3 — the skies.** THE AURORA (the conductor: calls in rhythm bring the
burst; the sky fades and can be called back); THE JETPACK (dust, the last
ring slowed, the minaret framed); THE LANTERN (it dims, and can be relit
higher); THE ACQUA ALTA (a gondola in the flood; ride it out); THE
HELICOPTER (a second run; ring eight slowed and framed).

**M4 — the rest.** THE BALLOON (the altitude said at the rim); THE BIG
WAVE (the barrel's own seconds); THE CROSSING (the last lunge framed);
THE DROP (three rings of light on the way down, and a riser); THE ORCA
(escort cannot tick; the breach from the boat); THE GRAND PRIX (contact:
the pack shoves back; the podium's champagne).

A shared channel for the world answering: `game.firework(x, y, z, o)` — a
placed burst on systems' own instanced sparks, with its sound at its
position. The chapters that have their own keep them.

Every batch: the marquee's own probe (`qa/l5-*.js`), `qa/fuzz.js` started
in nineteen chapters, `npm test` green.

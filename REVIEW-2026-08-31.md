# Code review sweep — 31 Aug 2026

A scheduled deep review of the whole tree: six parallel passes (systems.js in
full; main/shared/capybara/environment/weather; npc/props/condor; a
known-pattern audit of all 19 chapter files; a deep read of venice/drift/hanoi;
the build/tooling layer). Every finding below was verified against the
surrounding code before it was written down; each carries a confidence tag.
This file is the companion to `ROADMAP.md` (which covers delivery — LICENSE,
hosting, touch); nothing here duplicates it.

**How to execute:** batches are ordered by player impact. Each batch is sized
for one commit. Verify with the headless QA harness and judge visuals from the
rendered PNG, not from frame-mean metrics — the instruments that lie are
documented in memory (`instruments-that-cannot-hold-a-line`,
`visibility-metrics-that-lie`).

Two notes from doing it. The picker keys only work **on the title card**, so a

1. **The confiscation beat is dead in all 17 locals chapters.**
   `src/npc.js:2531` — in `localOwnStep`'s "CAUGHT YOU" branch,
   `game.physics.dropOwned()` is called with no arguments; `physDropOwned`
   (props.js:2646) returns immediately on a falsy prop, and even with the prop
   it guards on `prop.held` (NPC-held), not capy-held. A local who catches you
   holding their prop can never make you drop it — they lunge and loop chase
   lines until the 10 s `npcOWN_OUT_T` ceiling quietly ends the state.
   **Fix:** when the player holds the prop, call `game.physics.release(null)`;
   keep `dropOwned(p, …)` for the NPC-owner case (that call site in `npcFumble`
   is correct). Verify: pick up an owned prop, let the owner catch you, confirm
   the mouth empties and a reaction line plays.

2. **Tab-switch unpauses the world behind the ledger/album.**
   `src/systems.js:19725` — the `visibilitychange` handler restores
   `game.state.paused = document.hidden || jrShown`, ignoring
   `ledShown`/`albShown` even though `ledShow`/`albShow` set `paused = true`.
   Open ledger → switch tabs → return: physics, incidents and the sfx gate run
   behind the modal. **Fix:** `|| ledShown || albShown` in the restore
   expression, mirroring `ledHide`/`albHide`.

3. **Gamepad LT fires slide and grab at once.**
   `src/systems.js:18505,18513` — `padSlide = padBtn(g, 6)` (v44) coexists with
   a pre-v44 `wantAction = padBtn(g, 2) || padBtn(g, 6)`. Every pad slide also
   grabs props / triggers E-interactions. **Fix:** drop button 6 from
   `wantAction` (X grabs, LT slides, RT runs).

4. **Train Street furniture folds and never unfolds.**
   `src/hanoi.js:2203` — the fold rotation runs unconditionally
   (`lerp(rotation.y, f.fr, dt*3)`) instead of following `hanFoldK` the way
   position does; every stool/table/crate rotates to its folded yaw within ~2 s
   of chapter start and stays there. **Fix:** store the open yaw in the folder
   record and set `rotation.y = lerp(openYaw, f.fr, hanFoldK)`. Verify from a
   PNG before and after a train pass.

5. **Traghetto pontoons don't ride the tide.**
   `src/venice.js:3320-3333` — pontoons and mooring poles are merged static at
   build-time `venWaterY` (tide low) while the boat rides the live tide; at
   high tide the decks are ~2.3 m underwater and the boat floats 2 m above its
   stops. This is exactly what `venFloatM` (venice.js:260-265) exists to
   prevent — the rio topi and fruit barge already use it. **Fix:** author the
   pontoon/pole geometry into `venFloatM` with y=0 at the surface.

6. **The Sydney ferry is permanently off its berth.**
   `src/environment.js` (`envBuildFerry` ~1965-1980) — `envFerryPX/envFerryPZ`
   are never seeded (the van's twins are, at :1204), so the first
   `envFerryStep` computes a clamped (−12, 0, −12) m/s kick from origin, and
   because the drive is open-loop from-target the ~0.2–1.2 m offset never
   corrects: berth/gangway vs wharf edge alignment is permanently wrong.
   **Fix:** seed `envFerryPX = envFERRY_NODES[0]; envFerryPZ =
   envFERRY_NODES[1];` in `envBuildFerry`.

7. **One wall bonk permanently de-positions five capybara sounds.**
   `src/capybara.js:2273` — the bonk handler sets `capySfxAt.at = null`, but
   `capySfxAt` is documented (:570-573) as carrying a permanent reference to
   `capyPosition`; five later cues (soft wheek :2704, slide rustle :3001,
   blown-hop gasp :3494, shake-dry rustle :4134, whiff rustle :4442) set only
   volume/pitch, so after the first bonk they all go mono for the rest of the
   session. **Fix:** delete line 2273 (line 2271 is redundant too).

8. **The shuttlecock teleports between legs.**
   `src/hanoi.js:2690-2703` — `to` skips two players on alternate rounds but
   the next `from` is always previous-`from`+1, not previous-`to`; five legs in
   ten open with a ~3.3 m teleport and the kick fires on the wrong player.
   **Fix:** persist `from` as the previous leg's `to` instead of deriving both
   from the clock.

9. **Venice's tide re-bake wipes the breadcrumb trail.**
   `src/systems.js:13555,22469` — `mapBake` ends with `mapTrailClear()`
   (written for chapter swaps), but Venice re-bakes on every fifth of the tide,
   erasing the trail several times per cycle. **Fix:** only clear when
   `bio !== mapBakedFor`.

10. **Pasto speech bubbles age at 2×.**
    `src/npc.js:7736` + `:8539` — `updateBubbles(dt)` runs twice per frame in
    Pasto (end of `paUpdate` and again in `update()`'s `!biomeLive()` branch).
    **Fix:** drop the call inside `paUpdate`.

## Batch 3 — Ambient and latent bugs — **DONE, 31 Aug 2026**

| what | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| seaplane max per-tick move, one full 94 s cycle | **44.962 m** | **0.281 m** |
| failed build's partial left in the scene | **yes**, and a retry made it **2 copies** | **0** |
| spill candidates visible to the scan, standing in Sydney after a Kowloon visit | **20** | **1** |

The seaplane's measured 44.962 m matches the 44.9 m computed by hand from the
constants, at 6 m altitude outbound and 58 m on the way back, twice per cycle.
The circuit is authored, so it was not moved: she flies **on** to it over the
first tenth and **off** over the last. Attitude is damped as well, because the
same seam carried a 113 deg yaw snap and a 0→−0.30 roll step that were only
invisible while she was also teleporting.

The spill numbers are the mechanism, measured: a detached Kowloon's root is
`visible:false` but **107 of its 110 meshes still say `visible:true`**, of which
16 are bright emissives — because `Object3D.traverse` recurses unconditionally
and detach only touches the roots. Scoped to the live chapter the way
`sysEchoCast` already scopes its ray.

**The sun swap: confirmed real, deliberately not "fixed".** Measured sun
direction as drawn — Sydney 41.4°/145.9°, and Pasto, Cappadocia, Iceland and
Marrakech **all** 61.1°/157.9°. All nine `tall` chapters run Pasto's
near-noon equatorial star, whose own comment says it exists to stop the Plaza de
Nariño church tower throwing a slab across the square. Iceland's subtitle is
"half past eleven, and the sun is not the plan".

But the *fix* is per-chapter art judgement across eight graded chapters, and
picking a star for each of them blind would regress work that took many passes.
So the coupling is now **explicit and one line wide** — `sysSUN_BY_BIOME` —
seeded with exactly the star each chapter is already drawn under. Re-measured
after the change: **identical, to the decimal.** Giving Iceland its low midnight
sun or Cappadocia its dawn is now a table edit plus a look at the frame. **This
is the second Batch 7 candidate, alongside the rim clones.**

Also fixed: `wantDepth` now includes `p.airLight` (and the else branch zeroes
`uAirLitK`, which it never did); Hanoi's clip toast and wheek toast no longer
share one told-flag, so wheeking in traffic can no longer silence the chapter's
core instruction; five positionless `sfx()` calls given their speaker
(`startChase`'s whistle/gasp — the most frequent chase cue in the game — the
jogger's gasp, two streetdog wheeks, the church-bell gasp, and the gull mob,
which now plays at the chips); retrieval's give-up line drawn from a new
`npcLOC_GIVEUP` pool instead of the bystander `rush` pool; and the mangled
`envVanRideM` comment put back on the line it belongs to.

Soak over twelve chapters: **zero errors, zero NaN.**

**A third instrument that lies, found here.** Measuring a moving object across
`page.evaluate` boundaries reports real-time drift between calls as a one-tick
jump — it invented a 1.011 m "jump" in the fixed seaplane. Start each chunk from
the object's current position, never from the previous chunk's last sample.

## Batch 4 — Efficiency — **DONE, 31 Aug 2026**

| what | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| DOM mutations per 4 s, journal open, standing still | **27,840** | **4** |
| Venice swell attribute uploads per 600 ticks | **600** (60 Hz) | **300** (30 Hz) |

The journal number is the headline and it was far worse than the review
estimated: not 60 refreshes a second but roughly **6,960 DOM mutations a
second** — each refresh rewrites the header, the shelf caption, nineteen
souvenir slots and nineteen rows (tally, bar width, record line, three ARIA
attributes each), and setting identical `textContent` still replaces the node.
On a paused, motionless screen. The card is now rebuilt on open, where it always
should have been, and the per-frame path writes one string only when it differs
— which is once a second, because the clock is the only thing on that card that
moves. Verified it still *works*, not merely gone quiet: 19 rows populated on
first open, while held, and on reopen, with the clock running 0:04 → 0:07 → 0:09.

The Venice swell is 2,555 vertices × 3 sines plus a full attribute re-upload,
for a 5–12 cm swell whose slowest wave has a ~7 s period, in the heaviest
chapter in the game. Nothing reads the attribute back — it is display geometry —
so it now runs at the 30 Hz the Drift's identical cloud loop settled on. Checked
the swell is still alive afterwards: all 40 sampled vertices moving, 0.31 m of
travel over two seconds.

Also fixed: the **spill fade was applied twice in one frame** when a chapter
emptied (the early block damped every slot, then fell through to the hold/fade
loop which damped the same slots again), so a light going out took about half
the time it was tuned for — that one is a correctness bug, not a saving.
`biome.attach` no longer runs `world.bodies.indexOf` inside a loop over the
chapter's own bodies (~10⁵ comparisons twice per travel). The gull mob's
full prop scan is on a 0.4 s tick instead of 60 Hz. Hanoi's two independent
240-scooter nearest-scans are now one shared per-frame answer, and it early-outs
entirely when the animal is off the lanes — verified the beacon still answers
from both the lake and the traffic.

**Not done, deliberately: the event-rate `sfx` option literals** in
`capybara.js:4692` and `environment.js:1315/1657`. They fire seconds apart, not
per frame, so the saving is unmeasurable — and routing more call sites through
shared mutable scratch objects is *exactly* how Batch 1's wall-bonk bug happened
(one handler nulled `capySfxAt.at` and silently de-positioned five cues for the
rest of the session). Not a trade worth making for contract purity.

**A fourth instrument that does not resolve:** idle HUD mutations measured 873
before and 982 after — i.e. slightly *higher* after a change that only adds
write guards. The observer counts the whole HUD subtree, which churns with
whatever the chapter's crowd and ambience are doing, so run-to-run noise swamps
the stamina/arrow guards. Those two are correct and cheap, but this instrument
cannot see them; do not quote it as a win.

Soak over twelve chapters: zero errors, zero NaN.

## Batch 4 — Efficiency

the difference between headroom and luck on weaker machines.

5. `src/npc.js:8098-8105` — `qgStep` full-scans `game.props` at 60 Hz in idle

6. `src/systems.js:6546-6552` — emptied spill slots are damped toward zero

7. `src/main.js:586-590` — `biome.attach` runs `world.bodies.indexOf` inside a

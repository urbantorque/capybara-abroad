D10 (3 Sep 2026), self-directed after ROADMAP-DELIGHT's shelf emptied. Contract
section **THE NINETEEN-CHAPTER CHECK — D7 AND D8, EVERYWHERE**; instruments
`qa/d10-cover.js`, `qa/d10-hang2.js`, `qa/d10-clear.js`. Commit `e9f912d`.
Follows [[capy3-world-life]] and [[capy3-the-body-second-half]].

**THE RESULT IS A NEGATIVE ONE AND IT IS WORTH THE SAME AS A POSITIVE.** Both
batches were measured where they were BUILT — the hang in Sơn Đoòng, the flock
in the piazza, the beat in Kyoto, the act light in Monaco, the gesture in
Sydney, the herd in the Pantanal. Asking all six questions of all nineteen
chapters found **nothing dead**. That is the answer, and the sweep is cheap
enough (~4 min) to be worth running after any batch that ships a shared system.

**A DISTANCE CULL AND A DEAD SYSTEM ARE THE SAME READING.** The first sweep
stood at each chapter's SPAWN and eleven of nineteen hung things read 0.0000 —
including Marrakech under 3.6 units of wind. `physHANG_FAR` is 70 m and it
zeroes the pendulum on the way out; an exit board is rarely within 70 m of the
door you arrived by, and Göreme's is **122.2 m** away. From six metres all
nineteen swing (0.0095–0.7236 rad) and all nineteen answer a shout
(0.0681–0.4848). **Any audit of a system with a distance cull MUST report the
distance and the cull flag**, or a sweep taken from the wrong place reads as a
feature that was never wired. Same family as
[[capy3-instruments-that-cannot-hold-a-line]].

**`n: 0` MEANT TWO THINGS.** Göreme registers the flock's SCARE channel alone —
its four hundred cliff pigeons are one scalar `gorPigeonOut`, not four hundred
records — so `count`/`at`/`put` are absent and `flockDebug` read `n: 0`,
indistinguishable from a chapter whose birds have gone. `scareOnly` now says
which. **When an api has optional halves, the audit has to name which half was
registered.**

**THE CLEARANCE NOTHING HAD CHECKED.** Every hung thing is at
`boardY + topY − 0.10` and reaches `len` down, so clearance over the door's own
floor is `topY − 0.10 − len`. All nineteen positive, minimum 0.55 m (Göreme).
The four tightest — Göreme 0.55, Pasto 0.61, Cali 0.64, Hanoi 0.69 — are four
of the five **strands**, which are the ones deliberately long enough to walk
through, so a bottom edge at head height is the specification. `game.exitBoard()`
is what makes this measurable in one evaluate per chapter.

**Two negatives worth not re-deriving:**

- `carriedBy` is written in exactly THREE places — `condor.js`, `npc.js`,
  `palawan.js` — and D8 wired `hold` on all three. There is no fourth carrier
  still drawing the gardener's flail. See [[capy3-the-body-second-half]].
- **Sydney and Pasto have no beats and no anchored ambience, and that is not a
  gap.** `beatAudit` walks `locals`; those two chapters have none, their people
  being the instanced rosters. Giving a roster person a job is a different rig.
  Same distinction [[capy3-faces-and-bodies]] draws.
